## Earlier settled qualification decisions

[ADR0001](../adr/0001-autonomous-execution-and-qualification.md) and the prior MASTER qualification amendment remain intact. Their Q1–Q7 belong to the earlier qualification interview, separate from product Q1–Q35.

- Autonomous method choice within admitted scope; dependable and economical ordinary-work recipe, Fast when justified.
- 72 hours on a frozen release/configuration, at least30 distinct ordinary tasks across both repositories, at least100 actual experiment execution attempts. Generic retrieval/planning/evaluation jobs do not count as experiments.
- Preregister repository/task-family/campaign/recovery strata; queued-never-started jobs separate; actual retries count as experiment attempts but not additional ordinary tasks. Retain all failures, costs and outcomes.
- Mixed workload; coordinator restart, worker loss and committed lost response; queued/running/late cancellation; CPU/GPU coverage for declared backends.
- Zero lost accepted records, duplicate effects, unauthorized admissions, false acceptance or unplanned workflow repair. Candidate debugging, negative science and qualified planned recovery remain distinct.
- Measure baseline then freeze absolute and slowdown limits, p50/p95/max/sample counts, with provider waits separate. Poor baseline calls for improvement rather than weakened limits.
- Separate20 consecutive accepted ordinary-work trials. No selecting successes around failures; share raw evidence only where release/configuration/evaluator/workload/criteria protocols match, never count shared evidence as two independent observations.
