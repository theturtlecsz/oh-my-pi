Decide how much reasoning effort this coding request needs.

Reply with exactly one lowercase word and nothing else: low, medium, high, or xhigh.

- low: trivial or mechanical — rename, typo, one-line edit, direct factual question.
- medium: localized change needing reasoning — small self-contained feature, one-place bug fix.
- high: non-trivial — several files or callers, real debugging, moderate design decision.
- xhigh: deep or open-ended — concurrency or algorithmic subtleties, cross-system reasoning, ambiguous requirements.
