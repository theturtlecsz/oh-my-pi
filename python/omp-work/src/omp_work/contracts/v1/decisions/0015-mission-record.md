# 0015 — Mission record

## Problem

1. The mission is the unit that carries scope, budget, priority and progress across every downstream item, but nothing owns its status. "Where are we?" has no single source, and no written rule says which status changes are allowed or who may make them.
2. New or materially changed scope must reach the owner, while routine work inside scope must not. Without a written rule, a model would decide what counts as material and what needs confirmation.
3. A mission's budget is the envelope that item budgets (OMP-404) are drawn from. Without that link, an item budget is unbounded and an overrun has no holder.

## Decision

1. **One mission record.** WorkService adds the five mission commands — `submit_mission`, `revise_mission`, `approve_mission`, `set_mission_status`, and `link_mission_work` — over `MissionDraft {project_id, objective, constraints, acceptance_criteria, context_refs, artifact_expectations, requested_capabilities, repositories, approval_classes, risk_policy, approval_policy, effort_policy, budget_policy, priority, continuation_of, parent_mission}`. A `MissionDraft` is a draft's content; the record is the mission as held by the ledger. `created_by` and `created_at` are service-stamped, never sent by a caller. `risk_policy`, `approval_policy` and `effort_policy` are policy ids whose meaning belongs to OMP-417/OMP-418.
2. **Nine statuses, enforced transitions, recorded causes.** Owner decision D29 fixes the status set: `draft`, `awaiting_confirmation`, `approved`, `running`, `paused`, `blocked`, `completed`, `failed`, `abandoned`. WorkService refuses a transition outside the allowed set and records the cause of each accepted transition — a principal, a policy rule id, or a decision id. `SetMissionStatusPayload` carries the cause kind and only the id belonging to that kind; the other id field is refused. A requested target status is never `draft`, `awaiting_confirmation`, or `approved`: those are reached through submit, revise and approve, not a status set.
3. **Approved scope is a revision plus its basis.** `ApproveMissionPayload {mission_id, revision, basis_kind, basis_id}` binds one revision to a decision record Chris answered or the id of the project's standing mandate (OMP-418). The basis id is recorded, not verified here; OMP-414 and OMP-418 own answering and mandate identity. A mission inside its project's standing mandate needs no separate confirmation; a mission outside it waits for confirmation — which is why an approval for new scope is owner-gated (D29, D32).
4. **Materiality is a written rule.** A revision whose scope is new or materially changed under D29 (a) adds an outcome not in the approved criteria, (b) drops or weakens an approved criterion, (c) touches a repository or project outside the approved envelope, (d) needs a capability or D16 approval class the envelope does not include, (e) needs more than the approved budget, or (f) changes the objective beyond clarification — returns the mission to `awaiting_confirmation`; a revision inside the approved scope does not. Software applies the rule; a model may propose a classification and that proposal never changes the outcome. Anything the rule cannot decide is material, since software cannot tell a clarification apart.
5. **The mission budget is the envelope.** `budget_policy` is the mission's `ItemBudget`, read as the envelope from which item budgets (OMP-404) are drawn; item budgets cannot exceed it in total. A mission without a budget policy inherits its project's standing budget (the `standing_budget` key of the project's provenance); if the project has none, the mission is held with a decision record and no default budget is invented. Such a budget-held mission leaves hold only through a revision that supplies a budget, or through `abandoned` with the hold decision as cause.

## Consequences

- The contract bundle declares the `GET /v1/workspaces/{workspace_id}/missions/{mission_id}` read, the five mission command types, the `mission` read kind, and the `mission_transition_refused` and `mission_budget_exceeded` error codes.
- A mission touching outside its envelope is refused at approval, not at submit: submit records the draft.
- No migration is introduced. A mission is the `mission` snapshot in the result of its latest applied mission-command event in `omp_audit.domain_events` (aggregate id = mission id), as OMP-405 derives stop state.
