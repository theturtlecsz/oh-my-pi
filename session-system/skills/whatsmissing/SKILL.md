---
name: whatsmissing
description: Surface the user's blind spots about the current work. Use when the user wants to know what they haven't thought to ask, or asks "what am I missing?".
---

Stop and answer this question honestly, in plain prose:

**What's the biggest thing I'm probably missing about this that I haven't thought to ask?**

Rules for the answer:

- Step outside the framing of the current conversation. The best answer is usually something neither side has been treating as a question at all — an assumption silently load-bearing, a stakeholder or surface nobody mentioned, a second-order effect, a clock ticking somewhere.
- Ground every item in the actual work and codebase state, not generic risk lists. Point at the specific file, decision, deadline, or dependency.
- Rank by blast radius if several things qualify — biggest first — and say in one line why it matters and what it's sitting under.
- Distinguish "you haven't asked" from "we don't know": flag whether the gap is a question the user should ask, a fact nobody has verified, or a decision being made by default through inaction.
- If a cheap check resolves one of the gaps, run it instead of only naming it.
- If the honest answer is "nothing material — the obvious blind spots are already on the table," say that and show why, briefly. Do not manufacture drama.
- Plain language, no hedging. This skill exists to say the uncomfortable thing early.
