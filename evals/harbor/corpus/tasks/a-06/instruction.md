When our qualify-installed run cannot spawn its stage command, the error escapes and the staged release and its evidence path are discarded, so I have nothing to inspect afterward. A runner that throws never produced a result, so treat it as a stage failure: report the failure and keep the staged root and its evidence. The same applies to the pytest launch: a launch failure must be folded into a pytest-failure result that still preserves the staged release and its evidence path, and pytest must never run when the stage failed.
