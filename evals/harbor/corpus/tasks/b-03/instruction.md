Our JSON-RPC message reader trusts whatever a language server or debug adapter sends. A peer that sends an endless header, a huge or garbage Content-Length, or a header that arrives one byte at a time makes the editor burn CPU and memory, and the reader then stays in a half-broken state. A wrapper that prints log lines before the real frames also confuses it. Make the framing safe against hostile or broken peers: bound what it buffers, reject bad framing with a clear error, keep valid and split messages (including multi-byte text) working, and make the LSP mux and the DAP client react to the error instead of hanging.
